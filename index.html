About Me

Quality-focused. AI-powered. Results-driven.

I am a Senior QA Engineer with 8.5+ years of experience in software testing covering both manual and automation. Around 5 years have been dedicated to automation testing across Mobile (Android & iOS), Web and API layers.

I specialize in building scalable automation frameworks, defining test strategies, and integrating AI into daily QA workflows — from intelligent dashboards to scheduled automation pipelines.
